• camelCase: The first word starts with a lowercase letter, and every subsequent word starts with a capital letter. (Example: myVariableName)

• PascalCase: The first letter of every single word is capitalized. (Example: MyClassName)

• snake_case: All words are written in lowercase letters and separated by an underscore. (Example: my_variable_name)

• kebab-case: All words are written in lowercase letters and separated by a hyphen. (Example: my-variable-name)
